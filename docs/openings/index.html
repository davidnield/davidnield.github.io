Who plays your opening?

How often an opening is played, online on Lichess and over the board in top players’ classical games, split into White’s choices and Black’s — and how often you’d meet it compared with your opponent

Play a line on the board, or pick one of the 3,810 named openings below it, then choose the games: Lichess online games, by speed and rating, or classical over-the-board games among top players (the SuperGMs, or one of 47 leading players as White, Black or either). True reach is the share of games that reach the position by any move order. Because moves alternate, the share along your move order factorises exactly into W, the product of White’s choices, times B, the product of Black’s. The same factors answer a second question: if you always play the line, you get it whenever your opponent cooperates.
