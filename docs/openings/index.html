Who plays your opening?

Lichess opening popularity, split into White’s choices and Black’s — and how often you’d meet it compared with your opponent

Pick any of the 3,810 named openings. True reach is the share of games that reach its position by any move order. Because moves alternate, the share along the named move order factorises exactly into W, the product of White’s choices, times B, the product of Black’s. The same factors answer a second question: if you always play the line, you get it whenever your opponent cooperates.

logTicks = (vals) => {
  const v = vals.filter((x) => Number.isFinite(x) && x > 0);
  if (!v.length) return undefined;
  const lo = d3.min(v), hi = d3.max(v), span = Math.log10(hi / lo);

  const out = [];

Familiarity is not soundness. A big edge means you will have met the position many more times than the person across the board, which is a practical advantage, largest in fast games. It says nothing about whether the line is objectively good: several of the biggest edges belong to speculative gambits that engines rate as better for the defender.
